Privacy Policy for Number Strike

Effective Date: February 14, 2026

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Number Strike ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and retain information when you use our mobile app (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate gameplay, social features, and rewards.

A. Information You Provide

Authentication Data:

  • We use Firebase Authentication.
  • Supported sign-in methods include Email/Password (with email verification), Google Sign-In, and Sign in with Apple.
  • Guest/Anonymous login is not supported.
  • We process identifiers such as Firebase UID and, depending on provider, your email and basic account profile.

Profile Data:

  • Nickname (and normalized nickname key for uniqueness checks/search).
  • Profile photo (URL and, in fallback cases, photo bytes in Base64 form).

Camera/Photo Access: If you choose to change profile photo, the app may request access to your camera or photo library.

Social Data: Friends, friend requests, match invitations, invitation status, and basic social activity state (such as online/offline presence timestamp/state).

B. Information Collected Automatically

Gameplay and Competitive Data: Match records, match history, guesses/turn progression, results, mode stats (Single/BO3/BO5), rating/tier metrics, wins/losses, streaks, and credits balance/changes.

Push Notification Data: FCM device token and token refresh timestamps used to deliver notifications (for example invitations and credit-related notifications).

Technical/Diagnostic Data: Device/app/service metadata handled by our infrastructure providers (for service stability, fraud prevention, and debugging).

Advertising Data: For rewarded and banner ads, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK may process advertising identifiers and ad interaction signals under its own policies.

Referral and Deep Link Data: Referral code in invite links, temporary pending referral code stored locally until account onboarding completes, and referral attribution/reward status.

Local Device Storage: We use local storage (for example SharedPreferences) for settings and app state such as sound preferences, onboarding/hint state, and pending referral code.

3. Matchmaking and AI Opponents

To reduce waiting time, matchmaking may fall back to AI-controlled opponents when a human match is not found quickly. Matches against AI are handled in game systems similarly to other matches (including rating/stat and credits flows per current game rules).

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • run real-time gameplay, matchmaking, social interactions, and invitations;
  • manage credits economy, ad rewards, and referral rewards;
  • maintain leaderboards and competitive integrity;
  • provide notifications you enabled;
  • secure accounts, prevent abuse/fraud, and enforce nickname uniqueness;
  • operate, troubleshoot, and improve app reliability and performance.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We share data only as needed with service providers and legal requirements, including:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Storage, Cloud Messaging);
  • Google AdMob (ad serving and related measurement);
  • Apple/Google authentication providers as required by your selected sign-in method.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect safety, rights, and integrity.

6. Data Retention

We retain data as needed to operate the service and preserve competitive integrity (including leaderboard/match-related records), unless deletion is required by law or valid user request.

Profile photos may be stored as URLs, and in fallback scenarios as Base64 bytes for continuity.

7. Account Deletion and User Rights

You can request deletion using the in-app Delete Account flow in Settings. When processed, we remove account/profile data we control for your user account (including auth/profile linkage and nickname reservation).

Some records may remain where required for system integrity, legal obligations, fraud prevention, backups, or data that has already been incorporated into other users' records/aggregated history.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us for requests.

8. Children's Privacy

Number Strike is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such data, we will take appropriate deletion steps.

9. International Processing

Our service providers may process/store data in regions outside your local jurisdiction.
