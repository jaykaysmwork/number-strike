Privacy Policy for Number Strike

Effective Date: February 8, 2026

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Number Strike ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a real-time PvP gaming experience, manage game economy, and maintain competitive leaderboards.

A. Information You Provide to Us

Authentication Data: We use Firebase Authentication for Guest (Anonymous), Google, and Apple Sign-in. We collect your Firebase UID and, depending on the provider, your name and email address.

Profile Information: You may choose to provide a nickname and a profile photo.

Camera and Photos: With your permission, we access your device's camera or photo library to allow you to set a profile picture.

B. Information Collected Automatically

Gameplay Data: We record match results, turn history (guesses, strikes, balls), rating (Elo), tier index, and win/loss statistics.

Technical Data: Our service providers (Google Firebase) may collect device-specific information (e.g., OS version, hardware model), IP addresses, and log information for security and service stability.

Advertising Identifiers: To support our "Rewarded Video" and "Banner Ad" features, we use the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK, which may collect Advertising IDs (e.g., IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android) and device interaction data.

3. Matchmaking and AI Opponents (Disclosure)

To ensure immediate gameplay availability, Number Strike utilizes a hybrid matchmaking system:

Human-to-Human: The system prioritizes matching you with other human players.

AI Fallback: If a human opponent is not found within 5 seconds, you will be matched with an AI-controlled "Bot."

Treatment of Data: AI opponents are treated as standard users within our database. Match results against AI affect your rating and are displayed on leaderboards identically to human PvP matches. Credits are consumed for matches against AI in the same manner as human matches.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To facilitate real-time, turn-based PvP and synchronization.
  • To manage the in-game economy (Credits) and grant rewards via ads.
  • To maintain competitive leaderboards (Single, BO3, BO5 modes).
  • To prevent fraudulent activity and ensure nickname uniqueness.
  • To provide support and respond to user inquiries.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Storage: Data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore, Storage, Auth).

Fallback Storage: If cloud storage is unavailable, profile photo bytes may be stored in Base64 format within our database to ensure profile persistence.

Retention: Gameplay logs and profile data are retained to preserve global ranking integrity. We do not automatically delete match history.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Google Firebase: For infrastructure, database, and authentication services.
  • Google AdMob: For serving banner and rewarded advertisements.
  • Legal Necessity: If required by law or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Number Strike is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover such data, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

8. User Rights and Data Deletion

Under certain laws (such as CCPA in California), you have the right to access or delete your data.

Current Process: Users may request account and data deletion by contacting us at the email address below.

How to request deletion: Send an email to jaykaysm@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address or account identifier associated with your Number Strike account. We will process your request and delete your account and associated data in accordance with our retention practices.

Data deleted: Upon request, we will delete your profile (nickname, photo), gameplay history, and account credentials. Some anonymized or aggregated data may be retained for legal or operational purposes.

Future Updates: We are working on an in-app "Delete Account" feature to automate this process.
